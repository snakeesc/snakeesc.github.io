Recommended additions — in priority order

Code review, not a listening test. “No dedicated cue” does not mean the event cannot overlap other sounds. These are design recommendations, not new audio files or implemented features.

1. Finish the existing button-click wiring

High priority · reuse buttonClick3.mp3 — Play, Scores, How to Play and My Frogs & Stats currently omit playButtonClick(). Add consistent feedback to navigation, Close/Back, pagination, Save and Play Again where missing. Existing upgrade clicks already have feedback: avoid doubling it.

Use a quieter, short click on activation, not on hover. Respect mute. Verify the first tap works on Android; catch blocked playback without breaking the button. Save should click immediately, with success/error feedback only after the result.

2. Communicate important changes

Snake shed — high priority. A short rising hiss/rattle when the snake sheds and speeds up. The existing shed routine has no dedicated audio call. It matters more than another decorative sound.
Additional snake — high priority. A distinct brief danger cue. Despite the name handleFourthShed(), the timer invokes it every THIRD shed interval. Avoid piling a shed cue, spawn warning and upgrade cue on top of each other.
Run complete — high priority. A short closing sting separates game over from ordinary frog deaths. The end-run flow has no dedicated completion cue; play once, not every time someone reopens the summary.
Personal best — optional. A modest celebratory variation on run complete when a saved best is genuinely beaten; do not announce it before a score is accepted.

3. Fill the upgrade/role gaps

Crowned frog upgrade — medium priority. grantStarUpgrade() lacks a dedicated cue. Add a small ascending chime so it feels like a reward, matching the other audible orb outcomes.
Cannibal, Necromancer and Alchemist — medium priority. Their role-grant functions lack the dedicated grant sounds provided for Champion, Aura, Shield, Magnet, Lucky and Zombie. Distinct short variants would help identify what happened.
Grave Wave — medium priority. One spectral arrival cue per wave, rather than one sound per ghost. Prioritize it below menu and danger feedback.
Special upgrade selection — optional. Current choices reuse permanentBuffChoice.mp3. A richer variation for shed/epic rewards could distinguish them, but every individual upgrade does not need its own sound.

4. Consider only after the essentials

Shield break or final-frog danger. Brief cues can help players notice a survival change. Add a cooldown and trigger on the transition, not every frame or bite.
Buff expiry. Consider one subtle cue for a major defensive buff ending. Avoid sounds for every timer: overlaps would quickly become noise.
Save error / success. Pair quiet confirmation with visible text. Distinguish a rejected name or connection error from a successful save.

What I would leave quiet

Mouse hover, passive aura/magnet effects, every repeated Alchemist orb tick, and individual ghost spawns. Existing orb-spawn and buff sounds may already cover part of these events. Add ambient music only as a separate creative decision, not to fill a supposed missing-file requirement.

Suggested first pass

Wire existing menu clicks, then add shed, additional-snake and run-complete cues. Test the mix at a busy late-game moment with sound enabled and muted. Afterwards, fill the missing role/crown cues. This provides useful feedback without making the swarm exhausting to listen to.
