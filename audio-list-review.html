The entry hook is included in escape-the-snake-entry-audio-corrected.zip. The repository currently lacks that hook. It is triggered when the new snake reaches the playfield, not when it is created offscreen. Snake Egg now reduces speed gained during later sheds; its old baby-snake option is commented out.

Remaining design choices

  • Ordinary snake shed: loop remains intentionally disabled. The entry cue above is a separate one-shot in the prepared update.
  • Deathrattle revival: no dedicated success sound. Frog-death and other spawn sounds may play, depending on the cause.
  • Eye for Eye: survivors play the normal munch sound as they eat the fallen body; the snake’s death has no dedicated sting.
  • Grave Wave: no single dedicated wave cue. Avoid a sound for each individual frog.
  • Shield break or final-frog danger: optional, short cues if gameplay testing shows these events are easy to miss.

No additional MP3 is required for the current wired events. Suggestions above are options, not missing file errors. The old audio/ copies and the game-assets/audio/ runtime assets can differ; this review plays the latter.
