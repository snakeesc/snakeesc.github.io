Mixing in the prepared update

  • Major cues (shed, new snake, Grave Wave, Eye for Eye, run complete, personal best) briefly quiet routine ribbits, orb spawns, munches, and revival chimes for 0.85 seconds.
  • Molt Fortune’s batch of 5–10 orbs produces at most one orb-spawn cue per 0.18 seconds across both spawn variants.
  • Deathrattle revival is mixed at 40% volume and limited to one cue per 1.2 seconds, so a wave of revivals does not chatter.
  • Eye for Eye body bites use the existing munch sample at 45% volume and at most once per 0.28 seconds.
  • Grave Wave is 60%, shed is 65%, new snake is 75%, and Eye for Eye’s activation is 80%. Actual perceived loudness depends on the MP3 recordings.

These settings are included in escape-the-snake-audio-mix-update.zip; the audition buttons play the raw files, so the page does not simulate in-game mixing.

Remaining design choices

  • Ordinary snake shed: one-shot is prepared; the older continuous loop stays disabled. New-snake entry uses its own file.
  • Deathrattle revival: dedicated subtle cue is prepared for successful replacement spawns.
  • Eye for Eye: dedicated activation cue is prepared. Survivors still play the normal munch sound as they eat the fallen body.
  • Grave Wave: one dedicated cue per successful wave is prepared, including the third shed.
  • Shield break or final-frog danger: optional, short cues if gameplay testing shows these events are easy to miss.

The prepared hooks require your five event MP3s at game-assets/audio/. The ZIP does not include those recordings. Remaining suggestions are options, not missing file errors. The old audio/ copies and the game-assets/audio/ runtime assets can differ; this review plays the latter.
