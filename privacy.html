Privacy Policy — Escape the Snake

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Escape the Snake ("the game") is an arcade survival game. This page explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The game does not require an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information.

Information the game collects

  • Player tag. A short display name shown on the leaderboard. You can type your own, or the game will assign a randomly generated one (e.g. "PixelHop"). This is not tied to your real identity.
  • Run stats. When you finish a run, your score, survival time, and similar in-game statistics may be submitted to our leaderboard so it can be displayed to other players.
  • Client identifier. A random identifier is generated and stored on your device (in local app/browser storage) so the leaderboard can recognize your own entries across sessions. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and it is not shared with third parties.
  • Basic technical data. Our server (hosted on Cloudflare) automatically sees standard web request information, such as an IP address, when your device contacts it. This is used only as a fallback to prevent abuse of the leaderboard (for example, if a client identifier is missing) and is not stored in readable form — it is immediately converted to a one-way hash. We do not use it to track you across sessions or identify you personally.

What the game does not do

  • No account creation, sign-in, or password is required or collected.
  • No advertising SDKs, ad tracking, or third-party analytics are included in the game.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or other device data is requested. The Android app requests only internet access, which is required to load the leaderboard.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Leaderboard data (player tags and run stats) is stored using Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers and KV storage) so scores can be shared across all players. No other backend or third-party service is used.

Your choices

You can play the game entirely offline without submitting a score to the leaderboard. If you would like a leaderboard entry associated with your player tag or client identifier removed, contact us using the email below and we will remove it.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no account, name, or contact information is ever requested, no personal information is collected from any user regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
