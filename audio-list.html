Missing Audio (no file exists)

Found by reading every game event that has a sound, then checking which comparable events don't. These are real gaps in game-js/07-game.js, confirmed by reading the actual trigger functions — not guesses.

confirmedSnake shed (snakeShed())
The snake growing faster and changing color stage — one of the most dramatic recurring events in the game — has no sound at all, even though the much smaller "snake bites a frog" event does.
confirmedSecond snake spawns (handleFourthShed())
Part of the same shed mechanic (triggers on the 4th shed) — a whole extra snake appearing on screen, also silent.
confirmedPermanent star-upgrade orb pickup (grantStarUpgrade())
Orbs that level up a frog's star rank (speed/jump boost) play no sound on pickup, unlike every other orb outcome — temporary buffs play a buff sound immediately, and permanent build choices play permanentBuffChoice.mp3.
