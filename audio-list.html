Missing Audio (no file exists)

Found by reading every play*() call in game-js/07-game.js and checking which comparable events/roles don't have one. All confirmed in the code, not guessed.

Special frog roles with no sound (compare to the 6 role sounds above — these grant the same way but stay silent):

Cannibal — markCannibalFrog(). Gets its own emoji badge (🦴) like every other role, but no sound.
Necromancer — grantNecromancerFrog(). Granted from the same "choose a role" pool as Champion/Aura/etc.
Alchemist — grantAlchemistFrog(). Drops an orb every 12s, silently.
Ghost — markGhostFrog() / spawnGhostWave(), from the "Grave Wave" epic upgrade. A wave of ghost frogs appears each shed with no sound cue at all.

Game events with no sound:

Snake shed — snakeShed(). The snake speeding up and changing color stage is silent, despite the much smaller "bite a frog" event having a sound.
Second snake spawns — handleFourthShed(), triggers on the 4th shed. A whole extra snake appearing, also silent.
Permanent star-upgrade orb pickup — grantStarUpgrade(). Every other orb outcome has a sound (temp buffs, permanent choices) — this one, which levels up a frog's stars, doesn't.
