
MAGNETIZED (COMMON)
- Available once per run when at least three living Magnet frogs are on the field.
- Guaranteed one slot in the next Common menu while eligible. When Higher Calling replaces a shed's Common + Epic with two Epics, it can occupy one of those Epic slots instead. Never appears in consecutive upgrade menus.
- Selecting it sacrifices all Magnet frogs without triggering death/revival effects.
- All remaining and future frogs permanently pull orbs within 45 game units; existing Magnet frogs keep their 220-unit role range.
- New shaded blocky magnet sprite: game-assets/sprites/approved/upgrade-magnetized.png.

PROMOTION (COMMON)
- Moved from Epic to Common. Promotes a random 5–10 eligible frogs one crown level; fewer only if fewer eligible frogs exist. Skips frogs already at the crown cap.

LUCKY ROLL
- Duration bonus rises with each use in a run: +50%, +75%, then +100% for the third and later uses. Card uses the same short sentence and updates only the percentage for the next use. Resets on a new run.

MAGNETIZED ICON
- Replaced with the approved last preview, based on the existing Magnet frog artwork.
